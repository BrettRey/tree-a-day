## Caption (draft)

> "Not" here negates only "so", inside the complement, and the clause stays positive. So it's still an order, to bring about a world where it isn't so. "Don't make it so" would just cancel the order.

The grounding is CGEL's subclausal negation: in *Not long afterwards, he moved to Bonn*, "not applies only to modify long within the temporal adjunct ..., the clause itself being positive" (p. 95).

## Analysis notes

- **"so" as P** (Brett's ruling, 2026-10-30). CGEL has pro-predicative *so* functioning as "predicative complement" (p. 1537) but classes anaphoric *so* "simply as a pro-form" with no part of speech (p. 1538). Pro-form is a semantic category, not a syntactic one (Brett). Adv is ruled out because "adverbs cannot function as predicatives" (p. 529), which CGELBank's validator enforces. As P, *so* is an intransitive preposition, like deictic *here* and *there*.
- **"not" inside the PP**, as modifier of *so*. No gold precedent for a modified pro-predicative *so*.
- **Imperative with no Subj.**
