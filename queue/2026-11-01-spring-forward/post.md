## Caption (draft)

> "Spring forward, fall back." Traditional grammar calls forward and back adverbs. CGEL counts them as prepositions that take no object, like "in" in "come in".

CGEL p. 614: "There are good grounds for putting words of this kind in the preposition category"; list [30] includes *back* and *forward(s)*. As the only complement of an intransitive verb, *back* is Comp, not Particle (p. 280: "we will not invoke the category in intransitive clauses").
